How the numbers work

Levels to go uses each pet's exact XP. From level 100 to 200 every level costs the same 1,886,700 pet XP, so a Lvl 137 that is partway to 138 needs fewer than 63 levels.

Profit is the cheapest Lvl 200 BIN (no candies, no skin) minus the dragon's price. Auction house fees are not taken off. Profit / level is profit divided by levels to go.

Only BIN listings are ranked in the top 5. Regular auctions are tagged Auction in the full list and show their current bid, which can still go up. New marks a listing that appeared in the last 15 minutes. Use Copy to get a /viewauction command you can paste in game.

NPC profit: the NPC price is 50 Enchanted Gold Blocks and one each of Perfect Amethyst, Jade, Sapphire, Amber and Topaz Gemstones at bazaar instant-buy prices, plus 500M coins. Profit is the cheapest clean Lvl 200 BIN minus that. The dragon starts at 0 XP, so profit / level uses the same 1,886,700-XP levels as the table: 214M XP to Lvl 200 counts as about 113.4 levels.

Bazaar: the buy order is the highest price someone is bidding, which is what you get if you instant-sell. The sell order is the lowest price someone is asking, which is what you pay if you instant-buy.

Pet XP tracker: a GitHub Actions job runs every 15 minutes, even when nobody has this page open. It reads the pets menu of each player in config.json and saves the XP of every Golden Dragon there below Lvl 200, showing each player separately. Each player is checked once per run. The same job saves bazaar prices and Golden Dragon prices for the 7-day lines.

MP Leaderboard: magical power is the highest the player has reached on their selected SkyBlock profile, as Hypixel's API reports it. The same background job checks it every 15 minutes. This week is the change over the last 7 days of saved checks. The players are listed under mpPlayers in config.json.

Updates: the auction house API refreshes once a minute and the bazaar about every 20 seconds. The page checks right after each refresh, re-scans every auction every 5 minutes, and pauses while the tab is hidden.
